# Market preset: Australia 🇦🇺  (this is the prism base default)

**Header:** Name + one contact line (phone | email | LinkedIn). **No photo.**

**Do NOT include:** photo, date of birth, age, marital status, nationality/visa
(unless work rights are a genuine question — then one short line), religion,
a "references" section (write nothing; "available on request" is optional).

**Length:** 2–3 pages is normal and accepted.

**Tone / bullets:** Achievement-oriented. Verb + what + measurable result.
Australian/British spelling (organise, optimise, programme→program for code).

**Section order:**
1. Professional Summary / Profile
2. Professional Experience
3. Teaching & Learning / Leadership (if relevant)
4. Education
5. Technical Skills
6. Awards & Recognition

**Engine notes:** use `engine/prism-base.tex` as-is. No `graphicx` photo block.
This preset matches `examples/australia-sample.tex`.
