# Market preset: China 🇨🇳  (中国)

**Header:** Name + contact line. **Photo is common** — professional headshot
top-right via a `minipage` + `\includegraphics`. Enable `graphicx` (already in engine).

**Personal info line (常见):** may include age/birth year, hometown (籍贯),
years of experience. Political status (政治面貌, e.g. 中共党员) ONLY for
state-owned enterprises / government roles — otherwise omit.

**Language:** default **bilingual 中英对照** or a Chinese-first version for
domestic roles; keep an English version for foreign/JV companies. Chinese section
headings: 个人简介 · 工作经历 · 教育背景 · 专业技能 · 荣誉奖项.

**Length:** compact, often **1 page** for early career; 2 pages senior.

**Tone / bullets:** results + scale (团队规模、增长%、覆盖用户数). Keep concise.

**Engine notes:** add a photo block in the header; switch section names to Chinese
(or bilingual). Consider a CJK-capable compile (Overleaf: use XeLaTeX + `\usepackage{ctex}`
for Chinese text — note this in the generated file's top comment).
