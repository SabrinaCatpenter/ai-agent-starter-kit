# Market preset: Germany 🇩🇪  (Lebenslauf)

**Format:** tabular, two-column **date | content** layout. Very structured and
factual — German recruiters expect completeness and chronology, no gaps.

**Header:** "Lebenslauf" title. **Photo top-right is still common** (Bewerbungsfoto),
though legally optional since AGG — include unless the student prefers not to.

**Persönliche Daten block:** name, address, phone, email. Date/place of birth and
nationality are traditional but optional today — include if the student is comfortable.

**Section names (German):** Berufserfahrung · Ausbildung · Kenntnisse & Fähigkeiten
· Sprachen · Auszeichnungen. (Or English equivalents for international employers.)

**Sprachen:** always list languages with CEFR levels (A1–C2, Muttersprache).

**Bottom of page (important):** **Ort, Datum + Unterschrift** — place, date, and a
signature line. This is expected on a German CV.

**Length:** 1–2 pages, reverse-chronological.

**Engine notes:** replace the centered header with a left-aligned Persönliche Daten
block + photo; convert experience entries to a `tabularx` date|content layout;
append `Ort, den <Datum>` and a signature line before `\end{document}`.
